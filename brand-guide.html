01 · Foundations

What the brand is

The Healin Hub connects people with vetted aesthetic and self-care providers — med spas, injectors, salons, post-op care — across all 50 states. The brand promise in one line: every provider here has been looked at by a professional, and the one you need is closer than you think.

Credible

Trust is the product. Verification is visible, claims are specific, and the interface never overpromises. Proof before adjectives.

Effortless

Finding care should feel as light as receiving it. Generous white space, one action color, short paths from search to provider.

Warm

This is self-care, not surgery scheduling. Soft sage and blush keep the clinical edge off; the serif brings a human voice.

02 · Logo

The wordmark

The lowercase wordmark with its swoosh is the one element carried over from V1 — it’s friendly, owns the lowercase “healin” spelling, and reads at small sizes. It appears in exactly two colorways.

Colorways

Deep Moss on light — default in product
White on Spruce 700 — marketing moments, footer
Ink on Sage Tint — section bands
White on Spruce 900 — dark footers only

Clearspace: the height of the “h” on all sides. Minimum width: 140px on screen, 30mm in print.

Never: recolor the swoosh separately, set the mark over photography, add effects, or typeset “The Healin Hub” in the serif as a logo substitute.

⚠ Asset caveat: the supplied file is a 225×48px raster. A vector source (AI/SVG/EPS) or 4× export is required before hero-scale use.

04 · Typography

Instrument Serif + Figtree

Instrument Serif is elegant but modern — beauty without the boudoir. It carries display moments and headings down to 32px. Figtree does everything else: titles, body, UI, numbers. Both are open-source (Google Fonts), so the brand costs nothing to license.

The italic is the accent. One italic phrase per display headline, colored Spruce, lands the emotional beat: “Real results. Near you.” Never italicize body copy for emphasis — use weight.

Serif never goes small. Below 32px, switch to Figtree. Instrument Serif has one weight (400) — never fake-bold it.

Sentence case everywhere except the tracked uppercase label style. No title case, no all-caps serif.

Numbers are sans. Distances, counts, ratings, and prices are always Figtree — tabular, scannable, honest.

05 · Voice & tone

Straight-talking, warm underneath

The voice earns trust the same way the design does: by being specific, brief, and human. Second person always. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no wellness-speak.

  • Lead with proof.Counts, distances, verification before adjectives: “128 vetted providers within 10 miles.”
  • Short sentences. Plain words.“Near you,” not “in your area.” “Free to list,” not “complimentary.”
  • Trust is shown, then said.The badge does the claiming; copy explains vetting in one line, once per page at most.
  • Warm, not gushing.“Good care, close to home” — never “your journey to your best self starts here!”

06 · Iconography

Light lines, no decoration

Soft geometry, gentle float

10 · Photography

Morning light, honest skin

Imagery shows people and places you can actually visit — credibility over fantasy. Drop real photography below to test the grade.

Mood: morning light, clean air, calm confidence — real providers in real spaces.

Grade: bright and neutral with a whisper of sage in the shadows; no heavy filters, no clinical blue.

Never: stocky before/afters, syringes as hero imagery, or photography under the wordmark.

11 · Don’ts

How First Light goes wrong

✕Don’t decorate with Spruce.Spruce means “you can act here.” Decorative green panels dilute every button on the page.
✕Don’t add new accent colors.No purple, no teal, no gradient buttons. Warmth comes from Blush, depth from Moss.
✕Don’t let paid look like trusted.Featured (blush) and Vetted (spruce) must never share a shape, color, or position.
✕Don’t shrink the serif.Instrument Serif below 32px gets thin and fussy — switch to Figtree.
✕Don’t crowd.If a layout feels empty, that’s correct. Resist filling white space with stats, icons, or banners.
✕Don’t hype.No exclamation marks, no “transformations,” no “#1 directory.” Specific beats superlative.
