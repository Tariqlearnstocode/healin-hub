## 1. Project & Scope

Provider will design and build a custom rebuild of **The Healin Hub** — a nationwide searchable directory for aesthetic and self-care providers — replacing the Client's existing ListingPro/WordPress installation.

The engagement is **Quote A — The Working Directory**, a complete, launchable directory. Scope of work (the "Deliverables"):

1. **Geo-search** — every listing geocoded; distance and radius search sorted by real miles.
2. **Location search** — city, state, and zip resolve geographically (not text match).
3. **Faceted filtering** — category, service, location, and distance, with autocomplete.
4. **Results view** with live map pins beside the list.
5. **Provider profiles** — photos, services, hours, contact, socials, map, and lead form.
6. **Submit a listing & claim a listing** (with verification).
7. **Business accounts & provider dashboard.**
8. **Admin dashboard** — approve, edit, or remove any listing; manage categories and users.
9. **SEO landing pages** — category, location, and category+location.
10. **Supporting pages** — about, contact, pricing, privacy, terms, and system pages.

**Included with the build, at no additional charge:**
- The full **First Light** brand guide and design system, delivered and yours to keep.
- A user-friendly **admin backend** the Client can operate without a developer.
- Fully responsive (phone to desktop) build.
- SEO foundation — server-rendered pages, structured data, sitemap, clean URLs.
- Secure, isolated deployment — the Client's own database and modern hosting.
- A tier-ready data model (free listings at launch; paid tiers can be switched on later without a rewrite).

Listings are **free for providers** at launch.
