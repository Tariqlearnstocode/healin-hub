Why this works

Both colors live in one gradient

The gradient-forward feel isn't one flat color — it's teal easing into her pale mauve and plum tints. Deep teal on one end, soft lilac on the other, nothing saturated in between.

teal-600 → teal-200 → orchid-100 → plum-100 · every stop is from Dawn's original palette

One honest caveat

Why not flat teal-500 as the whole background?

Flat teal-500 behind text fails contrast — and hides your buttons.

White body text on teal-500 lands at ~3.4:1 (below the 4.5:1 readability bar), and since teal-500 is also the button color, buttons would vanish into it. So teal works in three registers instead: light teal gradients for the airy background feel, teal-500 for actions, and deep teal or aubergine for the dark anchors. Same intent the client asked for — teal in charge — without losing legibility or her purple.

Execution map

What to change in the code

The exact edits to flip the live pitch site to this direction. Palette scales in tokens/colors.css stay as-is — we only re-point the aliases and re-skin a few sections.

tokens/colors.css
Surfaces go teal-tinted & light. --surface-page: var(--n-50) → var(--teal-50)  ·  --surface-tint: var(--plum-100) → var(--teal-100). This is the airy teal ground under the whole site.
tokens/colors.css
Accent (default map pins, small marks) becomes teal. --color-accent: var(--plum-700) → var(--teal-600). --color-action is already teal-500 — leave it. Add a --featured-pin: var(--orchid-500) token for the one mauve pin.
index.html (.pitch-hero)
Lighten the hero. Replace the heavy linear-gradient(teal-500 → teal-700) with the two-radial light ground (teal-100 + orchid-100 over near-white). Flip hero text from white to --ink / --ink-soft, and the kicker/rule to teal.
css/pitch.css (.cta-band, .pitch-footer)
Keep one aubergine anchor. Leave --plum-900 on the footer for depth; swap the CTA band to deep teal teal-700 → teal-900 with a soft-lilac button. her purple stays
css/pitch.css (.demo-map .pin, .lp-minimap .pin)
Pins re-color. Default pins use the new teal accent; give the featured/vetted pin --featured-pin (orchid-500). This is the only saturated-mauve fill on the site.
brand-guide.html
Update the guide to match. Reorder the palette so teal reads as the lead, note aubergine as depth + mauve as tint-only, and log the change in docs/delivered-features.md.
